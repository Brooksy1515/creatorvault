What We Are

What CreatorVault is

The calm home base for creators who make more content than they can keep track of.

CreatorVault connects every product, sample, script, filming session, raw clip, AI clip, edit, post, and result so creators always know what exists, what is ready, and what needs attention next.

Why we exist

Because lost content becomes lost income.

Content finally has one home.

Raw clips, edits, AI assets, captions, and draft backups are saved where the creator can actually find them.

Every product keeps its full history.

Sample status, delivery, filming, scripts, clips, posts, and performance stay connected.

Great content gets reused.

One strong clip can become a TikTok, Amazon post, Reel, Short, ad, or seasonal repost.

Daily habit

Open Command Center first.

It tells you what samples need attention, what clips are unfinished, what is ready to post, and what can be reused.

Golden rule

Never leave content in only one app.

If it is in CapCut, TikTok drafts, camera roll, downloads, or an AI tool, log it in the Vault so it has a home.

Best outcome

Every product has a content history.

You can see what you filmed, what you posted, what performed, and what to make next without starting over.

Free and paid version

Start organized for free. Upgrade when CreatorVault starts saving you real time and content.

The free plan should help a creator finally get their products, scripts, and posts in one place. The paid plan should feel like the protection layer for serious creators who batch film, use AI clips, work with samples, and cannot afford to lose drafts.

Free

Creator Starter

$0

For creators who need one calm place to start tracking content.

  • Track up to 25 products
  • Basic sample status: requested, shipped, delivered, ready to film
  • Save scripts, hooks, CTAs, and hashtags
  • Simple Post Planner
  • Basic Vault with manual links and notes
  • Basic performance notes

Paid

CreatorVault Pro

$12/mo

For creators who batch film, sell affiliate products, use AI clips, and need content insurance.

  • Unlimited products, scripts, batches, and saved assets
  • Mass Import Inbox for camera roll, CapCut exports, AI clips, thumbnails, and voiceovers
  • Crash Rescue Queue for CapCut crashes, TikTok draft loss, missing shots, and refilms
  • Product Workspace that connects sample, script, batch, vault, post, and performance
  • Auto-tag suggestions by product, batch date, source app, and content type
  • Reuse Engine: turn one clip into TikTok, Amazon, Reel, Short, ad, or seasonal repost ideas
  • Advanced performance tracking: clicks, sales, winner notes, and what to remake
